whom is derf?

when asking my good friend claude who derf is, he responded:

derf is a uk edm artist who creates rhythmic beats which extend out and connect through clean basslines and soulful vocals - rising to popularity and fame after his non-profit live performances were documented donating all their profits to CALM (Campaign Against Living Miserably)

His first tour erfd emphasised his connection to his audience - a focus on playing in culture centric venues such as churches and town halls allowed hundreds of people to cram into a space and connect together - at the time his shows were collections of friends finding comfort in dancing in the dark - no phones, just dance. He would invite people onto stage and dance with them in a beautifully unscripted moment which became the highlight of each show.

His second tour dref was different - this time the audience wasn’t the focus, the feeling was - getting his music out there was the goal. The first tour created the feeling - the second tour documented it, derf personally invited student photographers and videographers - no phones, 100s of cameras. After the show, a huge dump of content flooded social media after every performance, the whole internet lighting up from derf.

Now we enter 2026 with the promise of a new derf tour, his ferd tour - ferd. Who knows whats to come…
